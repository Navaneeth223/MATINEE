S10 — Windows Volume Passthrough Test

Instructions

  1. Play a film in VLC on this computer
  2. Click Start Capture below
  3. In the browser prompt, select Entire Screen and enable Share system audio
  4. Lower the Windows volume slider (system tray)
  5. Watch whether the meter level drops
silence

Result Interpretation

If the meter drops when you lower Windows volume: The captured audio IS affected by the system volume control. Partner will hear volume changes when you adjust your speaker slider. This means Mode C requires you to keep Windows volume at 100% and control loudness via the Matinee mixer only.

If the meter stays constant when you lower Windows volume: The captured audio is NOT affected (it captures pre-volume-control). Partner will always hear full volume regardless of your local speaker setting. You can adjust your headphone volume without affecting what they hear.
